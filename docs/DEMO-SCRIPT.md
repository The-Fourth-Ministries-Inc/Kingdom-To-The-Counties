# 5-Minute Screen-Share Demo — Ambassador Companion (v1.16.0)

A timed walkthrough for showing the app on a screen share: to leadership, to a
new county's volunteer team, to a partner church, to a donor. **Nine segments,
five minutes**, each with what to click and what to say. The spoken lines run
about 700 words — a natural pace with room to breathe.

Read it once before you present. Don't read it *at* people.

---

## Before you share (2 minutes, off camera)

- [ ] **Open the app in a phone-shaped window.** Desktop Chrome → `F12` →
      the phone icon → **iPhone 14 Pro**. It's a phone app; showing it in a
      wide browser window makes it look like a website and the layout you're
      demoing isn't the one volunteers see. A real phone mirrored to the call
      is even better.
- [ ] **Unlock it before you share your screen**, not on camera. The Day PIN
      is the event's Saturday as `MMDD` and it's a live credential — don't
      type it in front of an audience you wouldn't hand it to.
- [ ] **Set your name** on the check-in page so the demo taps get credited to
      a real person and not a blank.
- [ ] **Decide leader or volunteer.** If you're unlocked as a leader you'll
      see the Dashboard and the leader-only controls; a volunteer audience is
      better served by locking those away first. Segment 8 is the only one
      that needs leader access.
- [ ] **Do NOT open the leader Quick Capture list on a shared screen.** It
      holds real people's names, phone numbers and voice notes. Segment 3
      shows the *capture* side only. Same for **♻️ Reset** and **🧹 Purge** —
      point at them, never tap them.
- [ ] **Second phone, if you have one.** Nothing sells the app like ticking a
      bin on your phone and having it appear on someone else's mid-sentence.
      Hand it to the person next to you before you start.
- [ ] **Have the trailer roster loaded** — open Specialists → Trailer Load
      List once while you still have signal, so the bins are cached.

## The five minutes

### 1 · Cold open — Event Day · `0:00–0:35`

**DO:** Start on the **Now** tab, scrolled to the top. Let the clock and the
"Now / Next" strip sit on screen for a second before you say anything.

> "This is the Ambassador Companion — what every K2C volunteer has in their
> pocket on event day. No accounts, no app store. It's a web page they save to
> their home screen, and everyone is looking at the same live board.
>
> This is home. It follows the clock: here's what's happening now, here's
> what's next, and underneath is the whole order of the day. Nobody refreshes
> anything. Nobody has to find a leader to ask what's happening at two
> o'clock."

**DO:** Scroll down through the order of events, then back to the top.

---

### 2 · Check-in · `0:35–1:00`

**DO:** Tap **Ambassador Resources → 🙋 Volunteer Check-In**. Type a name,
show the attestation checkbox, tap **Check in**.

> "First thing when you arrive: you check yourself in. Leadership instantly
> knows who's on the field and what team they're on — and from here on, every
> checkmark, every count, every capture is credited to your name automatically."

---

### 3 · Quick Capture — the heart of it · `1:00–1:50`

**DO:** Tap the **🧭 tab bar → Ambassador Resources → 📇 Quick Capture**.
Show the three lanes across the top. Tap the record button, say a sentence,
stop it. Then tap **✝️ Salvation** under "What was their response?"

> "Here's the one that matters most. You're on the field, you've just prayed
> with someone, and you've got maybe fifteen seconds before the next
> conversation. Three ways to capture them, whichever is fastest for you.
>
> Speak it — name, phone or email, everything you remember about the
> encounter. Or photograph the contact card they filled out. Or type it if
> it's quiet.
>
> Tap what happened — salvation, rededication, dedication, prayer — and
> submit. You're back out there in under thirty seconds, and leadership has
> what they need to follow up properly. Nobody gets lost on the back of a
> flyer in somebody's truck."

**DO:** Point at the sync pill.

> "And if there's no signal out there — which is most of a fairground — it
> waits on your phone and sends itself the moment the bars come back. You
> never stand still waiting for a spinner."

---

### 4 · Post — praise, issues, miracles · `1:50–2:25`

**DO:** Tap the **Post** tab. Show the four cards, then open **🙌 Miracle
Tracker**.

> "Post is the team's shared voice. Praise Wall for God moments, Report an
> Issue when something's wrong and leadership needs to know fast, and
> Announcements — a leader posts, and the headline drops onto every phone on
> the field.
>
> And this one is the season record. Salvations, rededications, healings,
> across all eight counties, in one place. Anyone can report what they saw —
> the person's name is optional on purpose. And it doesn't count until two
> *other* people who were there confirm it. That's the biblical standard, and
> it's enforced on the server, not on the honour system: your own report never
> counts as its own witness."

---

### 5 · Specialists + the Trailer Load List · `2:25–3:10`

**DO:** Tap the **Specialists** tab. Point at the cards as you name them.
Then open **📦 Trailer Load List** and type `gaff tape` in the search box.

> "Specialists is the work. Live attendance counter — several people counting
> at once, nothing double-counted. Setup checklist. Radio checkout. The
> 32-channel tech patch.
>
> And this. Both trailers, every bin, seeded from the logistics team's own
> sheet. Watch — someone's standing in the trailer and they need gaff tape.
> They don't know the bin number, so they type what's in their hand."

**DO:** Let the result render — bin 111, its trailer, where it rides.

> "Bin 111, Tiedowns, and it tells them which trailer and where it rides. Tap
> the box and it's on the truck — and it lights up on everyone else's phone
> within a few seconds. Something's missing? One tap flags it for the whole
> crew. Carrying the generator? Tap 'I've got this' so we know who has it next
> week."

---

### 6 · Pre-Crusade Mobilization · `3:10–3:40`

**DO:** Tap the **Pre-Crusade Mobilization** tab. Open one church.

> "This runs all season, before anyone sets up a stage. Every church in the
> region, who's aligned, who we've reached, and who on our team already knows
> somebody there.
>
> Call, text or email — and the email opens already written for that specific
> church, with the personal ask. Every touch is logged, so two ambassadors
> never call the same pastor twice on the same Tuesday."

---

### 7 · Studio and graphics · `3:40–4:05`

**DO:** Resources → **🎬 Recording Studio**, open a script so the teleprompter
shows. Back out, then **🖼️ Graphics for Sharing**.

> "Invite videos: every county has a script, and the phone is the
> teleprompter. Record it, send it to Laura, mark it done.
>
> And every volunteer is a marketing department — county flyers, wide banners
> built for a Facebook header, the Mission and Vision card, the promo video —
> download it and post it to your own feed."

---

### 8 · The Leader Dashboard · `4:05–4:35`

> Leader audiences only. Volunteer audience? Skip to segment 9 and give the
> time back to Quick Capture.

**DO:** Specialists → **📊 Leader Dashboard**. Scroll slowly through the tiles.

> "Leaders get one screen for the whole event: volunteers in, attendees,
> setup readiness, open issues, the load-out by trailer, decisions today,
> miracles this season.
>
> And nobody sets the Day PIN or switches the board between counties — it
> follows the schedule. The Monday after Sullivan, the app is running
> Grafton, and Sullivan's numbers stay exactly where they were."

---

### 9 · Close · `4:35–5:00`

**DO:** Back to the **Now** tab. Stop scrolling. Look at the camera.

> "It works with no signal, it syncs in seconds when there is signal, and the
> first time a new volunteer opens it, it offers to walk them around itself —
> so nobody spends morning huddle explaining an app.
>
> Point your phone camera at the QR code under Resources and you're in. See
> you in the field."

## Running long? Cut in this order

1. Segment 7 (studio + graphics) — 25 seconds.
2. Segment 6 (Mobilization) — 30 seconds, unless you're talking to pastors or
   partner churches, in which case it's the *last* thing you cut.
3. Segment 8 (dashboard) — only if the room isn't leadership.

Never cut segment 3. Quick Capture is the reason the app exists.

## The 60-second version

Open on Event Day (10s) → Quick Capture, record a voice note, tap Salvation
(30s) → Trailer Load List, search "gaff tape" (15s) → "works with no signal,
syncs in seconds" (5s).

## Questions you'll get

**"What if they don't have the app?"** — Resources → 📲 Share this App is a QR
code. Camera at it, done. They'll still need the Day PIN from morning huddle.

**"What if there's no cell service?"** — Everything they do queues on the phone
and sends itself later. The schedule, the trailer list and the booklets all
work offline from the last copy the phone downloaded.

**"Who can see the contact info we capture?"** — Only leaders, behind the
leader PIN. It's not in the data the app hands out to phones; it's fetched
separately and exported to Planning Center for follow-up.

**"Can somebody delete our stuff?"** — Removing an announcement, a miracle
report or a bin is leader-only, and it's checked on the server, not just
hidden in the app. Destructive actions snapshot a backup first.

**"Two people tapped the same thing at once — what happens?"** — They land on
the same answer. Every tap sends the final state, not a toggle, so a retry or a
double-tap can't flip it back.

**"How much does it cost to run?"** — It's a static page and one serverless
function on Netlify, with storage in Netlify Blobs. There's no separate
database to stand up, and nothing is licensed per volunteer.
