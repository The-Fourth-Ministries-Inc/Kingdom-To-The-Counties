Ambassador Companion Support

For volunteers and store reviewers

Ambassador Companion is the field app for authorized Kingdom to the Counties volunteers. It is published by The Fourth Ministries, Inc.

Get help

Email info@thefourthministries.com. Please include the app version shown in the header and whether you are on iPhone, Android, or the web app.

Day PIN

The app is locked to people serving that day. Your team leader reads the Day PIN at morning huddle. There is no public sign-up and no password reset. If you are a store reviewer, use the PIN supplied in the review notes.
